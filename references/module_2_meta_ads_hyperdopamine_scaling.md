# Module 2: Meta Ads Hyperdopamine Scaling — Creative-Is-The-Targeting & The 97% Market

*Source: Sabri Suby / King Kong, "How to Go From $0 to $100k/day with Meta Ads" (26:52). October 2026.*

### **2.1 Creative-Is-The-Targeting**

Modern Meta delivery algorithms no longer require narrow, stacked interest targeting. The **creative itself** — specifically the visual pattern interrupt and the opening **0.5-second hook** — acts as the automated filter that hunts and segments the audience: the right creative self-selects the right viewers and repels everyone else.

Implication: budget flows to creative volume and angle variation, not to audience-stack micro-optimization. An underperforming campaign is a creative problem before it is a targeting problem.

### **2.2 The Hyperdopamine Ad Formula**

Ads must break the scrolling trance:

- **Curiosity-driven headlines** — open a loop the scroll cannot close.
- **Raw native imagery** — mimic editorial news or candid UGC, never polished corporate ad aesthetics. The ad should look like content, because native camouflage is what earns the first second.
- **High-intrigue storytelling** — narrative tension carries the viewer past the hook.

Hook and pattern-interrupt copy engineering → `../../kirby-direct-response-copywriting/SKILL.md` §2.4; the 97/3 market doctrine behind the framing → §1.4 there.

### **2.3 Farming the 97% Market**

**97% of the market is not ready to buy right now**; only 3% is actively in-market. Direct sales pitches fight over the 3% — a finite, expensive, hyper-contested pool. Scaling to $100k/day requires converting the other 97%:

- Run **educational, native advertorials** (Module 4) rather than offer-first ads.
- Use **low-friction CTAs** ("Learn More") that invite the problem-aware and problem-unaware market into an education sequence instead of demanding a purchase decision.
- The direct offer appears only after the presell has educated, agitated, and demolished objections.

### **2.4 The 4-Stage Meta Campaign Pipeline**

| Stage | Name | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Validation** | Sandbox testing of hooks, angles, and creatives at low daily budgets |
| 2 | **Consolidation** | Funnel validated winning creatives into broad Advantage+ / CBO campaigns |
| 3 | **Vertical & Horizontal Scaling** | Incrementally scale budgets while deploying fresh creative angle variations |
| 4 | **Omnichannel Retargeting** | Retarget engaged users and harvest the search intent generated by top-of-funnel Meta impressions |

Stage 4 note: TOF Meta impressions generate downstream **search intent** — the harvest surface is Google Search, wired per Module 1. The retargeting pixel fires on the advertorial presell (Module 4 §4.3), not only on the offer page.
