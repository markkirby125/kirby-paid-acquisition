# Module 4: Advertorial Bridge Funnels — Native Presell Pages for Cold Paid Traffic

*Source: Sabri Suby / King Kong, "How to Go From $0 to $100k/day with Meta Ads" (26:52). October 2026.*

### **4.1 The Native Advertorial Presell**

Route cold clicks to **editorial pre-sell pages** — listicles, case-study articles, advertorials — that demolish objections **before** presenting the direct sales offer. The presell wears native editorial camouflage: it reads like news or candid content, not like an ad, because the click was earned by a native-pattern ad (Module 2 §2.2) and the page must honor that contract.

### **4.2 Low-Friction CTA Doctrine**

For the 97% not-ready-to-buy traffic — problem-aware and problem-unaware alike (Module 2 §2.3) — the presell CTA is **"Learn More"**-class — an invitation into education, not a purchase demand. The direct-response offer appears only after the presell has educated, agitated the problem, and demolished the standard objections. CTA doctrine and exclusivity-withholding rules for the offer page itself → `../../kirby-text-first-cro/SKILL.md` §6.

### **4.3 Funnel Topology**

```text
Paid click (Google Search — Module 1 / Meta — Module 2)
    → Native advertorial presell (this module)
        → Offer / bridge lander page
```

- **Never direct-link cold paid traffic to a sales page** — the offer converts only after the presell's objection demolition.
- **Never send paid traffic to a generic corporate homepage** — Module 1 §1.5's bridge-lander rule, generalized to every paid channel.
- The omnichannel retargeting pixel (Module 2 §2.4 Stage 4) fires on the presell, capturing the 97% who read but do not click through.
