# Module 1: Google Search Ads Mastery — Quality Score Trinity, STAGs & The Negative Keyword Shield

*Source: Sabri Suby / King Kong, "Give Me 24 Minutes and I'll Make You Disgustingly Good at Google Ads" (24:35). Frameworks derived from $122M+ in managed ad spend. October 2026.*

### **1.1 High-Intent Search Harvesting**

Eliminate broad, ambiguous search queries that attract tire-kickers. Campaigns must be structured strictly around high-commercial-intent modifiers — queries where the searcher is already looking to **buy**, **hire**, or **solve an acute problem**. Broad vanity keyword bidding destroys margins: it maximizes impressions among people who were never going to convert.

Audit rule: every keyword in every ad group must survive the question *"Is this searcher already in buying mode?"* If the query could plausibly be research, education, or idle curiosity, it belongs behind the negative shield (§1.3), not in the campaign.

### **1.2 The Quality Score Trinity (Golden Trinity)**

Google rewards relevance with cheaper bids. The arbitrage mechanism is an identical **1:1 message match** across three layers:

| Layer | Asset | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | User search query | The high-intent keyword the user actually typed |
| 2 | Ad copy headline | Near-verbatim echo of the query intent |
| 3 | Landing page H1 | Near-verbatim echo of the same intent |

An aligned trinity cuts Cost Per Click roughly **in half** while dominating top-of-page impression share (source claim from the $122M+ managed-spend framework) — Google prices confidence in relevance, and the trinity is how it is signaled.

Audit rule: for every keyword, read the query, the served headline, and the lander H1 in sequence. Any drift between the three is a Quality Score leak and a CPC premium.

### **1.3 The Negative Keyword Shield**

Most wasted ad spend comes from unvetted query matching. Install an extensive negative keyword list **before spending the first dollar** — proactively, not reactively (never wait for the search-terms report to reveal the waste):

- `free` — nobody spending $0 becomes a customer
- `jobs`, `salary` — job seekers, not buyers
- `DIY`, `template`, `course` — people avoiding hiring, or students

Extend per vertical with the same logic (`careers`, `internship`, `how to become`, `what is`, `examples`, `pdf`). The shield is maintained continuously: every search-terms review feeds new negatives back into the list.

### **1.4 Single-Intent Themed Ad Groups (STAGs)**

Dumping 50 varied keywords into one ad group destroys relevance — no single ad copy can echo 50 different intents, so the trinity (§1.2) can never align. Instead:

- Group campaigns into tight **3–5 keyword clusters** sharing identical commercial intent.
- Serve tailored ad copy per STAG, echoing the cluster's intent verbatim.
- Relevance compounds: STAG-level CTR feeds Quality Score, which feeds the CPC arbitrage.

### **1.5 Dedicated Bridge Landers**

Never send Google Ads traffic to a generic corporate homepage. Every STAG must land on a **dedicated bridge lander or lead funnel built specifically for that search query** — the H1 completing the trinity (§1.2). Funnel topology and presell architecture → Module 4 (cold paid traffic may route through a native presell stage *before* this lander — see §4.3 for the full sequence).
