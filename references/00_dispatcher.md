## When to Use
- You are structuring Google Search campaigns around high-commercial-intent queries and must engineer Quality Score via 1:1 query → ad headline → landing-page H1 message match (Module 1 §1.2).
- You are consolidating keywords into Single-Intent Themed Ad Groups (3–5 keyword clusters) or installing a negative-keyword shield before the first dollar of spend (Module 1 §1.3–§1.4).
- You are scaling Meta (Facebook/Instagram) campaigns: the creative-is-targeting doctrine, the Hyperdopamine ad formula, farming the 97% not-ready-to-buy market, or the 4-stage validation → consolidation → scaling → retargeting pipeline (Module 2).
- You are running Google Ads in a small town or low-volume geo (<50 searches/month): Presence-only location targeting, Target Impression Share bidding with a Max CPC cap, exact/phrase intent isolation, and the matched-locations audit (Module 3).
- You are routing cold paid traffic through native advertorial presell pages instead of direct-linking a sales page or a generic homepage (Module 4).
